# Codex review: F1 exit (2026-10-07)

Two auditors, run read-only against the committed files (diffed against the empty tree): **specification-gap-auditor** on `src/core/validate.ts`, `src/core/units.ts`, `schema/case.schema.json` and `schema/error-codes.json` against plans 02 and 04 (10 findings), and **silent-failure-hunter** on `src/core/conductivity.ts`, `hotDimensions.ts`, `limits.ts` and `heatCapacity.ts` (4 findings). One finding (the expansion range and the reference temperature) was raised by both, so there are **13 distinct findings**. Each was reproduced against the code before anything was changed; all 13 were real as described. Eleven are fixed, one is by design and documented, one is deferred and documented. The Codex run also exercised all 149 vectors (25 accepted, 124 refused as expected, no refusal returned a normalized case).

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | HIGH | A uniform transient start can have a negative or zero conductivity (a law positive only over its own range) | **Fixed**: `E_OUT_OF_RANGE` at `/transient/initialTemperature` for either solid |
| S2, N1 | MEDIUM | A linear law of 1e308 overflows and was accepted; `solveHot` returned a finite wrong temperature on overflow (300 K for 538.5 K, 813.98 K for 863.1 K) | **Fixed**: a conductivity must be 0.001 to 100000 W/m/K over its range (constant, linear, reciprocal, cubic, table), and `solveHot` throws on an overflow of the discriminant, the primitive or the bracket |
| N2 | MEDIUM | The discriminant of a huge cubic overflowed to NaN and skipped the interior minimum, so a law negative inside its range passed | **Fixed**: the three derivative coefficients are divided by the largest before the roots are taken |
| S3 | MEDIUM | Radii of 1e-200 m passed and gave non-finite temperatures | **Fixed**: a radius is 1e-9 to 1e3 m (`E_GEOMETRY`), a central hole smaller than 1e-9 m is refused |
| S4, N4 | MEDIUM | The expansion range warning ignored the reference temperature: the strain integral runs from it, so extrapolation below the range was silent | **Fixed**: the whole interval from the reference temperature to the operating end is checked (`W_EXPANSION_RANGE`) |
| S5 | MEDIUM | A conductivity failure inside hot dimensions was reported as `E_GEOMETRY` at `/hotDimensions` | **Fixed**: `hotGeometry` returns a typed cause; a conductivity failure is `E_OUT_OF_RANGE` at `/materials` as without the section, a non-positive coefficient is `E_OUT_OF_RANGE` at `/hotDimensions`, a closed gap stays `E_GEOMETRY` |
| S6 | MEDIUM | Reference temperatures of -1 K and linear ranges starting below 0 K were accepted | **Fixed** for conductivity and heat capacity, constant and linear |
| S10 | LOW | A source of whitespace satisfied "a source is required" | **Fixed** for conductivity, heat capacity and the gap |
| N3 | MEDIUM | A NaN temperature made a limit check pass (a NaN fuel state was skipped; a NaN cladding made a conditional rule "never in force") | **Fixed**: any non-finite state is counted, the check fails, and the note says so |
| S7 | MEDIUM | `hotDimensions` rejects an unknown key while the schema promises unknown-field preservation | **By design, documented**: a misspelt key in a physical input (`aplha`) must not pass silently; the schema description now says where fields are preserved (top level and material sets) and that `hotDimensions` is closed |
| S8 | MEDIUM | Plan 02 gave two outcomes for a law used outside its range | **Fixed in the plan**: a law not positive or not solvable is refused; a solvable case outside the stated range is run and flagged `out-of-domain` (plan 04 unchanged) |
| S9 | MEDIUM | An embedded material id of `!` passes `validateCase` while `validateMaterial` refuses it | **Deferred, documented**: imported materials keep their project's ids, which need not match the library pattern, so one rule at both entry points would refuse valid imports; the library enforces its pattern when a material is saved |

Tests: `tests/f1ReviewFixes.test.ts` (20), each written from the reproduction of its finding (the reproductions failed on the code before the fix); three older tests changed for the new rules (`tests/limits.test.ts`, `tests/reviewFixes.test.ts`, `tests/hotDimensions.test.ts`). 39 mutants of the fixes were run: 37 died; `zero allowed at the start` survived and got its own test, and `solveHot` base overflow unchecked is equivalent (the bracket check right after it catches the same overflow).
